Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated 2026

Who we are

Homes for Holiday is a holiday-home marketing directory established in 2014 and based in Devon, United Kingdom. We showcase exclusive holiday properties and connect travellers directly with property owners.

Information we collect

We only collect personal information that you choose to provide — for example, when you complete our enquiry form. This may include your name, email address, phone number and any message you send us. We use this information solely to respond to your enquiry.

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide the information you request.
  • To discuss listing your property or website-design services.
  • We never sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing.

Direct bookings & third-party websites

All properties featured are booked independently via the property owner’s own website. When you visit an owner’s website, or use our travel-partner links for flights and car hire, you are subject to that third party’s privacy policy. We are not responsible for the content or practices of external sites.

Cookies

This website uses only essential cookies required for it to function. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have provided. To do so, contact us at info@homes-for-holiday.com.
